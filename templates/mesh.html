What is P2P Fax Relay?

A decentralized mesh network where nodes cooperatively relay faxes to each other. Each node announces its delivery capabilities (modem, SIP trunk, email gateway) and the network automatically routes faxes through peers to reach any destination.

  • Kademlia DHT — distributed routing table maps phone prefixes to capable peers
  • mDNS Discovery — zero-config LAN peer discovery
  • Encrypted Relay — onion-style layered encryption through relay chain
  • Store-and-Forward — reliable delivery even when peers go offline
  • Trust Scoring — EigenTrust-inspired reputation prevents abuse
  • Cooperative Pool — N users × M faxes/day = N×M total capacity
